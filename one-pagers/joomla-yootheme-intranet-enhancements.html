The problem

Intranet content was inconsistent, in places off-brand, and in places broken. Department information was hard to find, org charts were out of date, and policy documents had no single home.

What was built

Department pages, org charts and portals, built to survive theme updates.

  • Department pages rebuilt as self-contained scoped HTML and CSS with prefixed class names, so they do not collide with the theme framework
  • Pages covering Payroll FAQ and process flows, Lean Six Sigma and Yellow Belt, Compensation and Benefits, Facilities, and the GTM roadshow
  • Policy and SOP portal with filterable cards plus bookmarking and recently-viewed, held in the browser
  • Org charts for TPMY and TPTH leadership, Office of the Chief of Staff, DST, Recruitment and Sourcing, Finance and GTM
  • Social media channels module and WhatsApp community directory
  • Brand audit and bug fixes on the internal job postings page, including a search index fix

How it runs

Joomla with YOOtheme Pro. Department pages are pure HTML and CSS with no JavaScript, built for desktop.
